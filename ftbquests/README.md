# Peaks & Talons — FTB Quests Progression

Target environment: Minecraft 1.21.1 NeoForge, FTB Quests 2101.1.36, FTB Library 2101.1.36, FTB Teams 2101.1.11, Curios API 9.5.1.

Quest configuration is authored for `config/ftbquests/quests/` and will be packaged with the playtest distribution.

## Chapter 1 — Peaks & Talons
Introduces chains, sockets, Curios necklaces, Eagle Feathers, and preparation for high-altitude exploration.

## Chapter 2 — Lord of the Skies
The Eagle encounter is a true exclusive branch.

### Path of the Hunter
Attack an Eagle -> defeat Phase I -> The Storm Arrives -> defeat the three Vengeance Eagles -> claim hunter rewards.

### Path of the Bond
Never damage that Eagle -> offer salmon -> survive repeated aerial taming attempts -> tame -> saddle -> first flight.

Attacking the Eagle permanently closes the Bond route for that encounter. Successful taming does not award Vengeance/combat-exclusive loot.

Both routes later converge into the Summit progression.

## Chapter 3 — The Summit Calls
Eye of the Eagle -> Eye of the Summit -> locate/activate Ancient Peak Shrine -> unlock the next mountain challenge.

## Chapter 4 — Master of the Peaks
Reserved for later Storm/Frost/Ancient Peak encounters, mount upgrades, and endgame artifacts.

## Detection strategy
Use FTB Quests item/kill/advancement/structure tasks where they accurately represent gameplay. Peaks & Talons custom advancements will represent mod-specific milestones such as choosing the Bond route, successful Eagle taming, surviving the Vengeance phase, first mounted flight, and shrine activation. This keeps quest completion deterministic and avoids manual checkboxes for important progression.
