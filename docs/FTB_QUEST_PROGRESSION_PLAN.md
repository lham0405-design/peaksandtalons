# Peaks & Talons — FTB Quest Progression Plan

Target: Minecraft 1.21.1 NeoForge + FTB Quests 2101.x

## Design rules
- Peaks & Talons is the primary adventure progression.
- Support mods teach useful systems but never gate the main Eagle/Summit story.
- Main Eagle choice is exclusive: Hunter OR Bond for an individual encounter progression route.
- Every new Peaks & Talons item must ship with item registration, inventory model, dedicated texture, localization, recipe/loot source, and quest icon/task wiring before it is considered complete.
- Use advancements/custom triggers for gameplay milestones when item possession is not sufficient.

## Chapter 1 — The Journey Begins
A broad onboarding chapter with several optional branches.

### Getting Started
1. A New World — obtain a crafting table
2. Stone & Flame — obtain stone tools and a furnace
3. Iron Age — obtain an iron ingot
4. Ready to Explore — obtain a shield, water bucket, and food

### Gearing Up
1. Suit Up — equip a full set of iron armor
2. Armed for the Peaks — obtain an iron sword or axe, bow, and shield
3. Take Aim — obtain arrows
4. Diamond Standard — obtain a diamond armor piece
5. Enchanting — create an enchanting table
6. Prepared Adventurer — optional completion milestone for armor + ranged weapon + food + healing

This branch is guidance/rewards only and does not block Peaks & Talons progression.

### Food & Cooking
Vanilla starter food leads into Farmer's Delight when installed.
1. A Proper Meal — cook a basic food item
2. Farmer's Delight — obtain/craft a cooking pot or skillet
3. Cutting Board Basics — use/obtain the cutting board
4. Hearty Meal — obtain a nourishing Farmer's Delight meal
5. Trail Rations — assemble several portable foods for exploration

Rewards should primarily be food, seeds, XP, or convenience items rather than progression artifacts.

### Useful Technology — Iron Furnaces
Optional compact tree:
1. Hotter Fire — craft an Iron Furnace
2. Faster Smelting — use/obtain the next practical furnace tier
3. Peak-Side Workshop — optional higher-tier furnace milestone

No Iron Furnaces task gates the main story.

### Village Helpers — Easy Villagers
Optional compact tree:
1. Meet the Villagers — interact with/obtain the mod's basic villager utility
2. A Safer Home — establish a useful villager setup
3. Trading Post — establish a trading workflow
4. Village Industry — optional automation milestone

No Easy Villagers task gates the main story.

### Whaleborne — optional side chapter/tree
Keep separate until exact installed 1.21.1 build/content IDs are confirmed. It will be exploration-flavored and optional, with no main-story dependency.

## Chapter 2 — Lord of the Skies
1. Signs Above — discover Eagle-related content
2. The Eagle — approach/discover a wild Eagle boss
3. The Choice — branch point

### Path of the Hunter (exclusive)
- Challenge the Eagle — damage/engage the wild Eagle
- Lord of the Skies — defeat Phase I
- The Storm Arrives — trigger Vengeance phase
- Three Against One — defeat all three Vengeance Eagles
- Spoils of Victory — collect combat-exclusive Eagle rewards

### Path of the Bond (exclusive)
- An Offering — offer salmon without damaging the Eagle
- Earn Its Trust — begin taming attempt
- Hold On — survive an aerial taming attempt
- Unbroken — complete the difficult multi-attempt tame
- A Golden Saddle — saddle the tamed Eagle
- Born to Fly — successfully ride/fly the Eagle

Hunter and Bond converge after their exclusive reward/milestone quests.

## Chapter 3 — The Summit Calls
- Eye of the Eagle / route-equivalent progression token
- Craft the Eye of the Summit
- Follow the Call
- Discover an Ancient Peak Shrine
- Activate the Shrine
- Unlock the next mountain challenge

Planned new items require finished visuals before test release:
- Eye of the Summit
- Storm Crystal
- Ancient Talon
- Golden Eagle Harness
- later shrine/boss artifacts

## Chapter 4 — Master of the Peaks
Long-term endgame chapter. Candidate progression includes multiple themed peaks, aerial mastery, upgraded Eagle equipment, and final summit content.

## FTB implementation notes
- Chapters should be grouped so primary story chapters are visually prominent and support-mod content is clearly optional.
- Use dependencies for linear teaching sequences.
- Use exclusive branching for Hunter vs Bond.
- Use item tasks for tangible crafting/collection goals.
- Use advancements/custom triggers for Eagle discovery, Vengeance start/completion, successful taming, first controlled flight, and shrine activation.
- Quest rewards should help the next step without trivializing boss encounters.
- Side-mod quests should remain valid if they are present in the pack, but Peaks & Talons must not require them for its own main progression unless explicitly changed later.
